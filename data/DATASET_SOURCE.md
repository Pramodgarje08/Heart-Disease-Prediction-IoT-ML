# Indian Cardiovascular Disease Dataset
Source: Doppala & Bhattacharyya, Cardiovascular_Disease_Dataset, Mendeley Data V1, DOI 10.17632/dzz48mvjht.1.
Official: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/dzz48mvjht/1
Kaggle mirror: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jocelyndumlao/cardiovascular-disease-dataset
The dataset is described as about 1,000 subjects from a multispecialty hospital in India and is CC BY 4.0.
Run `python ml/download_dataset.py` to download it. The ZIP does not fabricate patient records.
